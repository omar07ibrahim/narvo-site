05Measured, not promised

Tested on real Arabic speech, transcribed by people.

Five recognisers vote on every sentence, and the strongest three hear it again — slower, faster and denoised. A judge settles disputes with the meeting form at hand and may never invent a word. Every figure below was measured on our own GPU, in 8- and 16-bit precision.

  1. 5,458clips of real speech — Emirati, Saudi, news — behind every figure
  2. 6 / 8test sets where it beats the best single model — and it is never measurably worse
  3. 90%of recordings get the right quality grade, on datasets it was never tuned on
  4. 2–3×more errors found when a person checks the words it flags
