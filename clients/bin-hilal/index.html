Two tailored solutions for group finance

Two short films, about a minute each. One follows a handwritten invoice from a site photo to a record finance has checked. The other brings three companies on three systems into one group P&L you can trace back to every journal line. A person confirms every step that matters.

Concept 01 · Expense and invoice processing

One unclear number on a photo

1 min 10 s · English

What happens in the film

  1. Photo from site. An engineer photographs a handwritten invoice and tags it to Site A, pump maintenance. A printed receipt waits in the same queue.
  2. Photo left, draft right. Supplier, date, invoice number and two line items are read from the photo, each one boxed where it came from.
  3. One amount is unclear. It reads as 420 or 470, so it is marked for review and one click zooms into the original.
  4. Checked by a person. The reviewer confirms 420. The arithmetic closes: 500 plus 25 VAT makes 525. Project, category and the change are kept in the history.
  5. Handed to accounts. Approve for export gives an Excel file with Invoices and Line Items linked by one ID, the review status and a link to the photo.

Why it matters

Nothing unclear is guessed. When a figure could be read two ways, finance sees the original and decides, and the record keeps who decided and when.

Printed and handwritten documents, Arabic and English, go through the same review.

Concept 02 · Group financial consolidation

One figure in the P&L

1 min 11 s · English

What happens in the film

  1. Collect. October close: a journal from Odoo, a trial balance from another ERP and an Excel workbook. Duplicates and rows without an account code are held for review.
  2. Map. Two companies book the same cost under different codes. NARVO proposes one group line, site equipment expense, and the controller confirms the rule.
  3. Match. Both sides of an intercompany sale of AED 12,000 are matched and eliminated. A charge of AED 1,200 has no partner, so it stays in review and shows whose entry is missing.
  4. Report. The group P&L shows each company, the eliminations, the group total and September alongside. Click any total for the journal entries, the mapping rule and who approved the adjustment.
  5. Export. Every figure goes to Excel with a link to its source.

Why it matters

Group figures stop being a spreadsheet nobody can audit. Every number opens down to the line it came from, and every rule and elimination has a name and a date on it.

The companies keep their own systems. Nothing has to be migrated first.
